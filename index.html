Green ≠ guaranteed login

Green means the host responded. Some servers still need a login, registration, or your ISP's DNS. Use Open to go to the real homepage.

Mobile data is different

Most BDIX FTP servers only work on broadband Wi-Fi from a peering ISP. On mobile data, expect most private-IP servers to stay red.
